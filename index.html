Pokémon TCG Pocket Deck Builder & QR Generator

Pocket Deck Lab turns a decklist into the QR code that Pokémon TCG Pocket reads. Search for a card, tap to add it, pick your energy, and press Generate QR. Then, in the game, open My Decks → Build New → Scan Code and point your phone at the code, or scan a saved image.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Decks are saved on this device, imported screenshots are read locally, and nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts and no tracking.

How the deck code works

A deck code stores each card's in-game ID and your energy types. It doesn't store the artwork or rarity. The format was reverse-engineered and documented by Kevin Gutowski in tcgp-deck-qr; Pocket Deck Lab is a separate implementation of it. Card identities come from flibustier/pokemon-tcg-pocket-database, and card details from TCGdex and the Limitless card database.
